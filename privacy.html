Privacy Policy (English)

This policy explains how the web apps and sites published at mimimi-night.github.io (Simple Voice Memo Recorder, LookBookCheck, Name in other countries?, etc.) handle user information.

Information we collect

Email address when you create an account; payment status for paid plans (payments are processed by Stripe — we never store card numbers); anonymous usage statistics; and anything you send through the contact form.

How we use it

To provide the services (login, checking paid-plan status), to answer inquiries, and to improve the sites.

Recordings and photos

Voice recordings are stored only in your browser on your device. If you use AI summarization, the transcript text is sent to an external AI service to create the summary. Clothing items and photos in LookBookCheck are stored in the cloud (Google Firebase) as your own account data.

Google Firebase, Stripe, Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookie-less), Google AdSense, and Google Forms.

This site may use Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at www.aboutads.info.

Disclosure

We do not share personal information with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.
